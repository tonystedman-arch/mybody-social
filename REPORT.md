## Reading this

- **Saves** mean someone intends to come back. On a product account that is
  the closest free proxy for purchase intent.
- **Shares** are the only metric that compounds — a share puts the post in
  front of an audience you did not have.
- **Reach with no saves** means the hook worked and the payoff did not.
  Rewrite the middle, keep the first three seconds.
- **No reach at all** means the hook failed. Rewrite the first line.
